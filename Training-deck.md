# JSC Application Training Deck

## Slide 1: Title

**JSC Application User Training**

- Simple guide to using the full app ecosystem
- For HR, Payroll, Finance, Approvers, Admins, Auditors, and Staff
- Goal: help every user know what to do, when to do it, and where to do it

---

## Slide 2: What This App Does

**The app helps JSC manage people, payroll, approvals, payments, and reports in one place.**

- Keeps staff records
- Runs payroll
- Handles leave and staff requests
- Manages promotions and arrears
- Supports loans and cooperatives
- Controls approvals
- Tracks payments and reconciliation
- Produces reports and audit history

---

## Slide 3: Simple Big Picture

**Think of the app like one connected office.**

- HR enters and maintains staff records
- Payroll uses staff data to calculate salaries
- Approvers review and approve important actions
- Finance and cashier teams process payments
- Management reviews reports
- Auditors check history and compliance
- Staff use self-service features for their own records

## Slide 5: Who Uses What

**Different users see different parts of the app.**

- `Admin`: full access, users, settings, controls
- `HR/Staff Records`: staff data, departments, leave, requests, promotions
- `Payroll Officer`: payroll setup, payroll batches, arrears, payslips, reports
- `Reviewer/Approver`: approvals and decision making
- `Auditor`: audit trail, compliance checks, read-only review
- `Finance/Cashier`: payment processing and reconciliation
- `Loan/Cooperative Users`: loans, members, deductions, contributions
- `Staff`: self-service portal, payslips, requests, personal visibility

## Slide 6: First Things First

**This is the correct starting point for all users.**

1. Log in
2. Change password if required
3. Set up security features if enabled
4. Check dashboard
5. Read notifications
6. Open the right module for your role

**Simple rule:** do not jump straight into payroll or approvals without checking notifications and assigned tasks first.

## Slide 7: Business Process Map

**Main end-to-end flow**

```text
System Setup
   ->
Staff Record Created or Updated
   ->
Operational Transaction Happens
   ->
Review and Approval
   ->
Payroll or Payment Processing
   ->
Reports Generated
   ->
Audit and Compliance Review
```

**Simple meaning:** setup comes first, transactions come next, approvals happen before final payment, and reports plus audit confirm what happened.

## Slide 17: Module 8 - Approvals Workflow

**Approvals protect the organization.**

- Reviewer checks first
- Approver gives higher-level approval
- Auditor may review for compliance
- Users can approve or reject with comments
- Notifications alert the next person in line

**Why it matters**

- Prevents one person from controlling everything
- Creates accountability
- Keeps a clear decision trail

## Slide 26: Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Entering incomplete staff data
- Using wrong bank details
- Forgetting to check notifications
- Running payroll before setup is correct
- Ignoring arrears or adjustments before payroll approval
- Approving without reviewing details
- Locking payroll too early
- Skipping reconciliation after payment

---

## Slide 27: Best Practice Rules

- Start with clean setup
- Keep staff data updated
- Review before approving
- Use comments when rejecting
- Check totals before locking payroll
- Use reports to confirm outcomes
- Use audit logs when something looks wrong
- Follow role boundaries
