# JSC App Training Plan

## Purpose

This training plan is designed to prepare end users, operational users, approvers, and administrators to use the full JSC application ecosystem effectively. The flow starts with the most logical foundation: access, navigation, roles, and core records, then progresses into payroll operations, approvals, financial services, reporting, compliance, and self-service.

## Training Goals

- Help users understand how the modules connect across the full application.
- Build confidence in day-to-day transaction processing.
- Show the correct sequence for setup, operational work, approvals, and reporting.
- Reduce user errors by teaching role boundaries, dependencies, and best practices.
- Give each participant practical hands-on exposure in the modules relevant to their role.

## Suggested Participants

- System administrators
- HR and staff records officers
- Payroll officers
- Reviewers and approvers
- Auditors and compliance users
- Cashiers and payment officers
- Cooperative and loan operations users
- Staff portal users

## Training Approach

- Trainer-led demonstrations
- Guided hands-on exercises
- Role-based breakout practice
- End-of-day recap and Q&A
- Real-life scenario walkthroughs

## Pre-Training Preparation

- Confirm user accounts and role assignments are available before training.
- Prepare sample staff, payroll, cooperative, loan, and leave data for exercises.
- Ensure trainees know which modules they are expected to use after go-live.
- Share the training schedule in advance with expected attendance by role.
- Ask participants to bring real process questions from their department.

## Day 1: Foundation, Core Data, and Operational Flow

### Session 1: Welcome and System Orientation
- **Time:** 9:00 AM - 9:45 AM
- **Objective:** Introduce the app ecosystem, user roles, and the end-to-end business flow.
- **Topics:**
  - What the JSC app covers across HR, payroll, loans, cooperatives, banking, reports, notifications, and approvals
  - How different user roles interact with the same transaction at different stages
  - High-level process flow from setup to transaction to approval to payment to audit
  - Expected controls, responsibilities, and segregation of duties
- **Outcome:** Users understand the full ecosystem before learning individual screens.

### Session 2: Login, Security, Navigation, and Notifications
- **Time:** 9:45 AM - 10:45 AM
- **Objective:** Teach users how to access the system securely and move around confidently.
- **Topics:**
  - Login flow, password change, password recovery, and two-factor authentication
  - Role-based menus and what each role can or cannot see
  - Dashboard usage for daily monitoring
  - Notifications and approval alerts
  - Staff portal versus operational back-office views
- **Hands-On:**
  - Log in with assigned role
  - Change password
  - Locate dashboard, notifications, profile, and assigned modules
- **Outcome:** Every user can access the system and navigate to their work areas.

### Break
- **Time:** 10:45 AM - 11:00 AM

### Session 3: Master Data and Core Setup
- **Time:** 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM
- **Objective:** Establish the setup dependencies that drive downstream transactions.
- **Topics:**
  - Departments and organizational structure
  - Salary structures, allowances, deductions, and tax configuration
  - Payroll setup dependencies and why setup accuracy matters
  - System settings, workflow settings, and basic admin controls
  - User and permission management overview for admins
- **Hands-On:**
  - Review a department record
  - Review salary setup components
  - Review user roles and permissions
- **Outcome:** Participants understand what must be configured correctly before transactions begin.

### Lunch
- **Time:** 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM

### Session 4: Staff Lifecycle and HR Operations
- **Time:** 1:30 PM - 3:00 PM
- **Objective:** Train users on staff record management as the starting point for most business processes.
- **Topics:**
  - Staff onboarding and multi-step staff registration
  - Editing staff records and data quality expectations
  - Department assignment, salary details, and bank details
  - Staff status management and its impact on payroll eligibility
  - Promotions and how they affect salary changes and arrears
  - Leave management and staff requests
- **Hands-On:**
  - Create or review a sample staff profile
  - Update a staff record
  - Submit or review a promotion
  - Submit or review a leave request
- **Outcome:** Users understand how staff data feeds payroll, approvals, and reporting.

### Session 5: Payroll Concepts and Payroll Preparation
- **Time:** 3:00 PM - 4:15 PM
- **Objective:** Build payroll understanding before users run live payroll activities.
- **Topics:**
  - Payroll cycle overview from batch creation to locking
  - Payroll lines, earnings, deductions, taxes, cooperative deductions, and loan recoveries
  - Staff-specific allowances and deductions
  - Arrears concepts and when payroll must be regenerated
  - Common validation checks before submission
- **Hands-On:**
  - Review a sample payroll batch
  - Inspect payroll lines and adjustment impact
  - Compare payroll calculations for different staff scenarios
- **Outcome:** Users understand what drives payroll results and what to verify before submission.

### Session 6: Day 1 Practical Review
- **Time:** 4:15 PM - 5:00 PM
- **Objective:** Consolidate learning through guided practice.
- **Activities:**
  - End-to-end mini-scenario from login to staff update to payroll preparation
  - Role-based Q&A for HR, payroll, approvers, and admins
  - Recap of key controls and common mistakes
- **Outcome:** Participants finish Day 1 with a strong operational foundation.

## Day 2: Transactions, Approvals, Financial Services, Reporting, and Compliance

### Session 1: Payroll Processing End-to-End
- **Time:** 9:00 AM - 10:30 AM
- **Objective:** Train payroll users and approvers on the complete payroll process.
- **Topics:**
  - Creating payroll batches
  - Generating payroll lines
  - Reviewing exceptions and validating totals
  - Submitting payroll for approval
  - Approval stages and final readiness for payment
  - Payroll locking and the significance of finalization
- **Hands-On:**
  - Create a payroll batch
  - Generate lines
  - Submit for approval
  - Review the approval path by role
- **Outcome:** Payroll teams can run the payroll cycle correctly and understand approval dependencies.

### Session 2: Arrears, Promotions, and Adjustment Approvals
- **Time:** 10:30 AM - 11:15 AM
- **Objective:** Teach users how exception-based payroll items are introduced and controlled.
- **Topics:**
  - Promotion workflow and arrears generation
  - Arrears approval and merging into payroll
  - Staff adjustment approvals
  - Good practice for recalculation and validation
- **Hands-On:**
  - Review an arrears case
  - Approve or reject a pending adjustment
- **Outcome:** Users understand the controlled path for non-routine payroll impacts.

### Break
- **Time:** 11:15 AM - 11:30 AM

### Session 3: Loans, Cooperatives, and Member Services
- **Time:** 11:30 AM - 12:45 PM
- **Objective:** Cover the financial-service modules linked to staff and payroll.
- **Topics:**
  - Loan types, loan applications, approvals, guarantor flow, and disbursement
  - Loan repayment tracking through payroll
  - Cooperative setup, member registration, member transfer, and contributions
  - Registration fees, annual subscriptions, and monthly deductions
  - Cooperative reporting and operational controls
- **Hands-On:**
  - Review a loan application lifecycle
  - Register or review a cooperative member
  - Review a member transfer scenario
- **Outcome:** Users see how loans and cooperatives integrate with payroll and staff records.

### Lunch
- **Time:** 12:45 PM - 1:45 PM

### Session 4: Bank Payments, Cashier Workflows, and Reconciliation
- **Time:** 1:45 PM - 2:45 PM
- **Objective:** Train payment-processing users on the post-approval payment workflow.
- **Topics:**
  - Payment batches from payroll
  - Payment processing and execution workflow
  - Cashier dashboard usage
  - Bank statements, reconciliation, and exception handling
  - Payment status tracking and operational follow-up
- **Hands-On:**
  - Review a ready-for-payment batch
  - Review payment and reconciliation screens
- **Outcome:** Payment users understand how approved payroll moves into execution and reconciliation.

### Session 5: Reports, Audit Trail, and Compliance Monitoring
- **Time:** 2:45 PM - 3:45 PM
- **Objective:** Enable users to retrieve decision-ready information and verify accountability.
- **Topics:**
  - Standard reports and cooperative reports
  - Custom reports and saved templates
  - Exporting reports for operational use
  - Audit trail review for investigation and compliance
  - Best practice for report interpretation and traceability
- **Hands-On:**
  - Run a standard report
  - Review a saved report template
  - Inspect audit logs for a sample transaction
- **Outcome:** Users can generate reports and trace actions across the system.

### Session 6: Staff Self-Service and Communication Features
- **Time:** 3:45 PM - 4:15 PM
- **Objective:** Prepare staff users and support teams for self-service adoption.
- **Topics:**
  - Staff portal navigation
  - Payslip access
  - Leave requests, staff requests, and loan/cooperative visibility
  - Notification usage and follow-up expectations
- **Hands-On:**
  - Access the staff portal
  - Locate a payslip
  - Review self-service request options
- **Outcome:** Staff users understand how to use the self-service layer without depending on back-office teams.

### Session 7: Full Scenario Simulation and Wrap-Up
- **Time:** 4:15 PM - 5:00 PM
- **Objective:** Reinforce confidence through realistic role-based scenarios.
- **Scenario Flow:**
  - Staff record is created or updated
  - Promotion or adjustment is introduced
  - Payroll is generated and reviewed
  - Approval workflow is completed
  - Payment moves to execution
  - Reports and audit checks are reviewed
- **Wrap-Up Topics:**
  - Common user errors and how to avoid them
  - Escalation path for support issues
  - Go-live readiness checklist
  - Final Q&A
- **Outcome:** Participants see how the app ecosystem works as one connected platform.

## Training Materials to Prepare

- Slide deck with module overview and business process map
- User account list by role
- Sample transaction scripts for exercises
- Quick reference guides by role
- Attendance register and feedback form
- Post-training assessment checklist

## Recommended Delivery Notes

- Start each module with business context before showing the screen.
- Teach users the sequence of work, not just button clicks.
- Separate demonstration from hands-on time.
- Group participants by role for practice sessions where possible.
- Emphasize approval controls, audit visibility, and data accuracy.

## Post-Training Follow-Up

- Share role-based quick guides after the sessions.
- Run a short competency check for key operational users.
- Identify super users in HR, payroll, finance, and admin teams.
- Schedule a refresher session after initial live usage.
- Collect early support issues and convert them into FAQs.

## Expected Result After 2 Days

By the end of the two-day program, users should be able to:

- Log in securely and navigate confidently
- Understand their role and access boundaries
- Perform their daily tasks in the correct sequence
- Submit, review, approve, and monitor transactions appropriately
- Use reports, audit logs, and notifications for control and follow-up
- Support smoother go-live adoption across the entire app ecosystem
